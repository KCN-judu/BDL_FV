# Formal design decisions

One record per model choice of the formal development: what was chosen, what was
rejected, and the formal reason — a theorem, a counterexample or a definitional
argument in `BDL/`. A record is append-only once accepted: the frontmatter, a
dated `## Amendment`, and factual corrections (a wrong path) are the only edits.
A replaced choice is marked `superseded` and stays here; the kernel pages cite
only the replacement. IDs are `FVD-NNNN`, assigned in sequence and never reused
or renumbered; a record whose architectural question production has also decided
names the production record in its `production` field (`ADR-NNNN`, `PRP-NNNN`,
`ISS-NNNN`, with the relation `supports`, `audits` or `bears-on`), and current
documents cite the two together — `ADR-0032 (FVD-0118)` — so that one
architectural fact has one production identity and its formal evidence beside
it. The lifecycle and the fields are in
[governance.md](../project/governance.md); start a new record from
[TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and run `just docs-check`.

Records FVD-0001 … FVD-0130 were the entries of one numbered ledger until
2026-09-20; the permanent map from the old numbers is
[decision-id-migration.md](../project/decision-id-migration.md). Their text is
the ledger's, sectioned by its own `Rejected:` / `Reason:` / `Consequence:`
markers, and their dates are the commits that introduced them.
