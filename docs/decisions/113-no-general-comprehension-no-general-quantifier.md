# FVD-0113: No general comprehension, no general quantifier

## Status

Accepted in Phase 11.

## Decision

Nested binders cover the required cases (`exD`, `exE`); the forms are finite
list equations (`natural_forall`, `natural_exists`).

## Alternatives rejected

Generators/`yield`/`where`; `∀`/`∃` syntax.
